[R1] A marker is RAISED here when `avg_log2FC > 0`. Nothing else is a raise.

[R2] An unraised marker means one of two different things, and `pct_in` tells them
apart. Unraised with `pct_in` near zero is evidence AGAINST the identity: the cells do
not express what the resource says defines it. Unraised with a high `pct_in` and a
similarly high `pct_out` is not evidence against it; a cell type that makes up much of
the dataset drives its own defining markers to a flat fold change, because the
comparison is against the rest of the same tissue.

[R3] Publication counts order the reading of ONE identity's own panel; they do not rank
identities against each other. Within a panel, weigh the markers with the most
publications first. A high-`n_pub` marker sitting near zero outweighs a low-`n_pub` gene
with a wide contrast, because the second gene may be incidental to this identity while
the first is what defines it. One configuration reverses this: where an identity's single
best-published marker is undetected while several of its next-best are raised together,
the publication counts are describing the broader lineage the identity belongs to rather
than the identity itself, and the absent gene does not on its own refute it. Settle that
configuration on the sources for the absent gene.
